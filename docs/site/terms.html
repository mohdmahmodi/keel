05Your Mac and your data

Keel records each original before changing it and restores it when you switch a tweak off. Restoring depends on those records and on your Mac being in the state Keel left it. Keel may not be able to restore a setting exactly if the records in ~/Library/Application Support/Keel/ are deleted, if macOS or another app changes the same setting, or after a macOS update.

Cleanup deletes only the items you select. Deleted caches are normally rebuilt by the apps that own them, but deletion cannot be undone by Keel, and emptying the Trash is permanent.

You are responsible for your Mac, your files and your backups.

Keel is not liable for data loss, changed settings, system instability or other effects on your Mac that result from changes you choose to make.
